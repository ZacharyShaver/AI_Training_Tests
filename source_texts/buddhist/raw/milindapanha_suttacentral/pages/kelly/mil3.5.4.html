4. Question on Seeing the Dhamma

The king asked: “Venerable Nāgasena, have you seen the Dhamma?” “Throughout their lives, your majesty, disciples should proceed with the Buddha as their guide, the Buddha as the one showing the way.”

“You are clever, Venerable Nāgasena!”
