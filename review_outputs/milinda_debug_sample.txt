## mil3.1.1

Then, King Milinda said to Venerable N?gasena, ?How is the reverend one known, what is your name, venerable sir?? ?I am called N?gasena, your majesty.?

Then, Venerable N?gasena said this to King Milinda, ?Did you come on foot or rather in a vehicle?? ?I did not come on foot, venerable sir, I came in a chariot.?
